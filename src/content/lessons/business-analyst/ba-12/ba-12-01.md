> **Prerequisites:** ba-11 Real-world Case Studies

## Explanation

### Know the stages before you start

BA hiring processes vary, but most follow a similar pattern. Knowing each stage lets you prepare the right evidence for each one.

```
 Application → Screening call → Technical / competency interview → Case study or
 (CV + cover)   (recruiter/HR)   (hiring manager + BA lead)         assessment task
                                                                        │
                                       Offer ← Final / cultural interview
```

| Stage | Who | What they assess | Typical length |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CV / application** | Recruiter, hiring manager | Relevant experience, keywords, achievements | — |
| **Screening call** | Recruiter / HR | Motivation, salary, availability, basic fit | 15-30 min |
| **Competency / technical interview** | Hiring manager, senior BA | BA knowledge, techniques, examples from experience | 45-60 min |
| **Case study / assessment** | Panel | Applying techniques under time pressure; communication | 1-3 hours (sometimes take-home) |
| **Final / culture interview** | Senior leader, team | Values, collaboration, long-term fit | 30-60 min |

Smaller companies may combine stages; large organisations may add psychometric tests or a group exercise.

### The four question types

| Type | Example | What they're testing | Covered in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Conceptual** | "What's the difference between a BRD and an FRD?" | Knowledge of BA concepts | ba-12-02 |
| **Scenario / situational** | "A stakeholder keeps changing requirements. What do you do?" | Judgement and approach | ba-12-03 |
| **Behavioural** | "Tell me about a time you resolved a conflict." | Past behaviour as evidence | ba-12-04 |
| **Technical / tool** | "Write a user story with acceptance criteria." "How would you use JQL?" | Practical skills | ba-07, ba-09 |

### Preparing your CV for BA roles

| Do | Example |
|---|---|
| Lead with impact, quantified | "Mapped claims process; removed 3 duplicate checks, cutting processing time by 4 days" |
| Use BA keywords from the advert | Requirements elicitation, user stories, BPMN, Jira, stakeholder management, UAT |
| Show transferable experience | Customer service → understanding user needs; operations → process improvement |
| Include a projects section if you're new | Your case studies and assignments from this course |
| Keep it to 2 pages | — |

**Career changers:** every job has BA-like moments. Did you improve a process, gather requirements for a tool, run a meeting, train colleagues, analyse data? Use them.

### Research before the interview

| Research | Why |
|---|---|
| The company's products, customers and recent news | Shows interest; helps tailor examples |
| The job advert, line by line | Each responsibility is a likely question |
| Methodology (Agile, Waterfall, hybrid) | Prepare relevant examples |
| Tools listed (Jira, Confluence, Visio, SQL) | Be ready to discuss or demonstrate |
| Interviewers (LinkedIn) | Understand their roles and focus |
| Industry terms | Use them correctly |

### Build a story bank

Prepare **8-10 short stories** from your experience (or course projects) that you can adapt to many questions. Each should be in STAR format (ba-12-04).

| Theme | Example story |
|---|---|
| Eliciting requirements | Interviewed warehouse staff and found the real cause of picking errors |
| Stakeholder conflict | Mediated between sales and finance on approval limits |
| Process improvement | Mapped and improved invoice approval |
| Dealing with ambiguity | Started a project with no clear brief |
| Mistake / failure | Missed a stakeholder; what you learned |
| Influencing without authority | Convinced a manager to pilot instead of a big-bang launch |
| Working in Agile | Refined stories and supported a team through sprints |
| Data-driven decision | Used data to challenge an assumption |

### Questions to ask them

Always have 3-4 questions ready. Good ones:
- *"What does success look like for this role in the first 6 months?"*
- *"How do BAs work with Product Owners here?"*
- *"What's the biggest challenge the team is facing right now?"*
- *"How mature is your requirements practice: what tools and templates do you use?"*

Avoid asking only about salary or holidays at early stages.

## Real-world examples

| Organisation type | Typical process |
|---|---|
| Large bank | Online application → recruiter call → competency interview → 2-hour assessment centre with case study and presentation → final interview |
| Consultancy | Screening → case interview (live problem-solving) → partner interview |
| Tech start-up | Call with founder → take-home task (write user stories for a feature) → team interview |
| Public sector | Application with competency statements → panel interview with scored questions → sometimes a presentation |

## Step-by-step demonstration

**Scenario:** You've been invited to interview for a "Junior Business Analyst" role at an insurance company. One week to prepare.

**Day 1: Decode the advert.** It mentions: requirements gathering, process mapping (BPMN), user stories, Jira, stakeholder workshops, UAT support. Make a table of each with a story or example.

**Day 2: Research.** Read the company's annual report highlights, recent product launches and customer reviews (common complaints: slow claims).

**Day 3: Story bank.** Write 8 STAR stories. Link at least one to claims or customer experience.

**Day 4: Conceptual revision.** Revise key topics: requirement types, BRD/FRD, user stories and AC, MoSCoW, BPMN basics, Agile roles.

**Day 5: Scenario practice.** Practise answers to 5 scenario questions aloud.

**Day 6: Mock interview.** Ask a friend to interview you, or record yourself. Time answers (aim for 1.5-2 minutes each).

**Day 7: Logistics and questions.** Prepare 4 questions for them, test video setup, plan your route or login, rest.

## Practical example

Turn these job-advert lines into likely interview questions:

| Advert line | Likely question |
|---|---|
| "Facilitate workshops with stakeholders" | "Tell me about a workshop you ran. How did you prepare and what was the outcome?" |
| "Write clear user stories and acceptance criteria" | "Write a user story with AC for a password reset feature." |
| "Map current and future state processes" | "How would you approach mapping a process you know nothing about?" |
| "Support UAT" | "What's the BA's role in UAT?" |
| "Work in an Agile environment" | "How does the BA role differ in Agile vs Waterfall?" |

## Common mistakes

1. **Not reading the advert carefully.** It's a list of the questions you'll be asked.
2. **No prepared stories.** Inventing examples on the spot leads to vague answers.
3. **Listing tasks, not impact, on your CV.** "Wrote requirements" says less than "requirements cut rework by 30%".
4. **Ignoring the company.** Generic answers signal low interest.
5. **No questions for the interviewer.** It looks like low curiosity, a key BA trait.
